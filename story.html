01 / The Kaung Sett table

From Yangon,
with love.

Cooked slowly.
Remembered forever.

Kaung Sett means “good fortune.” It is the feeling of arriving at a table where someone has already made space for you.

Our kitchen draws from the streets, tea shops, and family homes of Myanmar. We keep the recipes generous and the room unhurried: bright herbs, toasted spices, and broths that ask you to stay a while.

Come for a bowl of mohinga. Stay for the stories.

Our door is open to everyone who comes hungry.
